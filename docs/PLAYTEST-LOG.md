# Playtest log

What the owner saw while playing, what we found, and what shipped. One entry per report, newest day first. A report
stays open until the fix is live on rtsai.net/play or the cause is shown to be outside the game.

## 26 October 2026 (owner, live page ae730bf, RA2 mode, the original Allied country)

| # | Report | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No music can be heard; the Now playing panel shows "Grid Assault" playing | The game produces sound: on the same build, served locally and started by script, the music bus carried a signal (level 0.066 RMS on both the music and the master bus, audio context running, clock advancing, track Grid Assault). The panel's "Now playing" state means the stream is active, unmuted and the context is not suspended. Silence after that point is the browser's or machine's output: the tab's mute, Chrome's site sound setting, the Windows volume mixer entry for Chrome, or the output device (an HDMI monitor without speakers). | closed: the owner confirmed on 26 Oct that the music was playing on another speaker |
| 2 | The Allied ore refinery is drawn over the docked ore truck; the truck should be on top | The depth buffer decides pixels, and the refinery sprite's depth offset (`Offset: -12, -52, 48` in sequences/allied-structures.yaml) put its bay wall about 2.5 px in front of a truck docked at DockOffset 1086,1086; a voxel's depth ignores ZOffset, so no unit rule could push the truck forward. Fix: the building's depth offset is 24 (RTSAI-Mod 75ba609), which leaves the wall 1 px behind the truck's ground point while the wings still cover units close behind them. | fixed in the mod; needs a data release |
| 3 | "Malformed rocks": a rock/rubble piece in a forest renders as a flat brown smear | The rock scenery on the shipped RA2 maps (TROCK/SROCK) draws 272x230 photo-real frames of editor-rocks.png at `Scale: 0.154`, which the web renderer minifies with nearest sampling into pixel noise, and with `ZRamp: 1` (a ground-decal depth ramp) so neighbouring tree canopies overdraw the rock's upper rows, leaving a flat strip. Fix: frames pre-resampled to their drawn size in their own sheet, Scale 1, no ZRamp (RTSAI-Mod 1cd56de; before/after in hq/games/artifacts/playtest-20261026/rocks/). | fixed in the mod; needs a data release |
| 4 | A trail of yellow ellipses behind a ship in water | `^Ship` leaves a `wake` trail whose sequence pointed at `conquer|wake1.shp`: the code-drawn placeholder (15 yellow ellipses, RGBA, so the effect palette cannot tint it). The project's own faint white wake, standalone/effects/wake1.png, was referenced by nothing. Fix: the sequence reads wake1.png (RTSAI-Mod c04eb60). | fixed in the mod; needs a data release |
| 5 | On a loaded map the undiscovered area's top edge is exposed: a strip of terrain shows above the black | Known engine behaviour on random maps: the generator keeps the playable bounds `1 + MaximumTerrainHeight` rows below the map's top edge (Terraformer.InitMap), but the rows above the bounds still get terrain with height, and a cell at height h draws h rows higher on screen. The top rows' high ground therefore draws above the shrouded area, where no shroud tile exists. Fix candidates: flatten the rows above the playable bounds in the generator, or clip terrain drawing to the projected map rectangle in the renderer. Both are engine changes and need a runtime release. | open, engine |
| 6 | Shots seem to land in the wrong place (Grizzly firing at an IFV, a fireball drawn at the Grizzly) | Measured on the same build (Grizzly `mtnk` against an IFV, a Rhino and a Chrono Miner, explicit attack orders, every effect draw paired with its projectile): 90 Grizzly shells landed a median 2 px (max 2 px) from the target and 151 px from the shooter; 95 IFV rockets a median 3 px (max 9 px) from the target; a chased moving IFV took all 5 hits at 2 px; against a miner that cannot shoot back, 45 of 45 shells hit it and nothing was ever drawn at the Grizzly. The fire seen on the Grizzly is the IFV's return fire: per duel about 10 rocket explosions and 3 to 6 crater puffs on the Grizzly against 5 shell explosions on the IFV, so the player sees two to three times more fire on their own tank. Receipts: hq/games/artifacts/playtest-20261026/impacts/ (summary.json, per-impact measurement.json, screenshots). | shots land on target; not a bug |
| 7 | (found while measuring 6) The Grizzly's muzzle flash never draws: 0 sprite draws keyed to the Grizzly across 70 shots although `WithMuzzleOverlay` is on `mtnk` and `gunfire.shp` is in sequences/defaults.yaml | to check: does the desktop game draw it (then the web bundle lacks the sprite or the sim's capture skips the overlay), or is the sequence not wired to the armament | open |
