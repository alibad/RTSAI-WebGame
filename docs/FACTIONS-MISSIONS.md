# Browser factions and missions

The public browser profile now exposes all sixteen countries in the RTS AI catalog: America, Germany, England, France, Korea, Cuba, Libya, Iraq, Russia, China, Iran, Türkiye, Saudi Arabia, Israel, Yemen and Hezbollah. Random faction pools include the restored countries.

The nine restored countries retain the mod's original unit rules, weapons, costs and signature units. Their browser rendering uses existing project-owned faction artwork and announcers. Shared infrastructure uses the standalone base kit. These are playable gameplay rosters; they do not introduce nine new bespoke art sets. Automated checks cover deployment, power production, technology prerequisites, signature availability, unit and structure creation, deployed GI/Desolator states, icons and snapshot rendering. They do not establish competitive balance.

## Missions

The launch menu offers Skirmish and Missions. Mission cards show a briefing, map preview, objectives and difficulty; in-game objectives include progress and target location buttons. Victories are recorded locally by difficulty.

Five missions are adapted from authored companion content:

- Jizan Corridor, Hodeidah Lifeline and Bab al-Mandab Passage: `OpenRA-AI/missions/red-sea-2026`.
- Haitan Network: `OpenRA-AI/missions/china-faction`.
- Straits Shield: `OpenRA-AI/missions/turkey-faction`, with the source map binary recovered from the Classic OpenRA packaged map.

Their source maps use the older RA terrain and actors. The browser ports preserve terrain layout, resources, important locations and core objectives, while replacing terrain tiles and units with compatible standalone RA2 project content. Objectives run in the deterministic simulation host because the browser build does not link the native Lua mission runtime. Relief vehicles and shipping likewise use available project units. These are adaptations, not byte-identical campaign imports.

Relay Under Siege is a new fictional Hezbollah training exercise, built on Twin Fords. No authored Hezbollah mission was found. It tests faction infrastructure, defense against waves and an attack objective. Old Classic campaign maps and internal AI fixtures are not exposed as compatible playable RA2 missions.

Two original chapters of Lines of Hope, Act III, are generated by `prepare-content.py` (`chapters()`) on the terrain of two adapted maps, with every actor placed in code. The generator checks each placement: buildings on land and off beaches and ore, ships and ferry waypoints on water, and a land route from Orion's wave entry to each target. Orion Group is the fictional contractor force; like the Obsidian Directorate in Haitan Network it uses the `russia` faction (unit art and the scripted wave type) and colour 545D66. The scripted waves (`Missions.Wave`) attack the player's oldest building still standing, so the order of the buildings in the generator sets where Orion strikes first.

- **Lights in the Water** (`lights-in-the-water`, Yemen, Captain Nabil Haidar): Hodeidah Lifeline's terrain with a reef coast painted along the south edge. A mobile force of 14 with no base must bring three units to each of three stranded groups (`SurvivorsCove`, `SurvivorsJetty`, `StrangerSite`; the stranger is the actor `Stranger`, on a non-combatant Survivors side whose original-country faction gives her the original commando art), each guarded by an Orion patrol (rifles, desolators, Tesla tanks), with a roadblock on the coast road. The transports can cross the reef only at high tide (a `survive` objective, 8:00); then three transports drive from `ReefLanding` along the coast road to `ClinicDoor`, and two must arrive. Meanwhile Orion's columns grind down the `WatchPost` line and then the town (north and west sites first): left alone, the clinic (`TownClinic`) falls at 11 to 13.5 minutes. Losing the clinic or every unit fails the mission. Map rules: the town's defences and generators do not count as the force, Orion's wave tanks (`htnk`) have 260 HP, and the transports (`sapc`) are slow (speed 50).
- **Hold the Ferry Line** (`hold-the-ferry-line`, Türkiye, Colonel Kemal Aydın): Straits Shield's terrain. A small port base with $10,000 and one refinery (its free harvester only) holds for 12 minutes. The columns take `NorthCheckpoint`, then `EastCheckpoint`, then `SouthCheckpoint` (one gun each), then the piers. Tanya (`Tanya`) fights with the defenders on her own allied side (faction `america`): a modern faction fielding her would change the original-art and original-voice overlays for everyone, because those generators follow who fields each actor, missions included. At 6:00 four ferries sail from the piers through the channel past two Orion destroyers (`OrionPicket0`, `OrionPicket1`); every ferry must reach the far shore. Losing either pier (`FerryPierNorth`, `FerryPierSouth`) fails the mission. Map rule: the ferries (`lcrf`) are slow (speed 50).

Balance was set with headless runs of the real simulation (`tools/mission-probe`: it boots `OpenRA.WebSim` like `websim run`, optionally attack-moves the whole force through named actors, and prints the objectives and cash each minute) and checked with browser playtests by the page's co-commander in Auto at 4x (`tools/chapters-playtest.mjs`; for Lights in the Water with `--director`, which sends the army to each objective in turn, because the co-commander does not read mission objectives). Hold the Ferry Line, co-commander in Auto: easy won twice, normal won twice (at 12:00), hard lost once (10:05) and won once; an idle player loses normal at about 9:50 and hard at about 7:40, and wins easy. Lights in the Water on normal: co-commander in Auto with the director won twice (9:35, 9:45); the co-commander alone (no director) never looks for the survivors and loses at about 7 minutes; an idle player loses at 11 to 13.5 minutes. Hard Lights in the Water is much harder (the scripted waves are six tanks every 34 seconds from 0:40): a scripted player loses at about 7 minutes. Human playtests remain the real check.

The last three chapters of Act III use the mission features below, in both hosts (`sim/OpenRA.WebSim/Missions.cs`;
the desktop trait `RTSAICampaign.cs` in RTSAI-Mod is generated from it by `tools/import-shared-campaigns.py`). Every
actor they place is checked against the standalone's stand-in art (`stand_in_art`): enemies are Orion Group
(`russia`, drawn with the original Westwood art in the isometric mode), and Tanya is the `tany` actor with
`Faction: america`, which gives her the original commando art and voice while the player owns her. The original-art
and original-voice generators (`tools/ea_originals.py`, which `ea_voices.py` follows) count a placed actor's own
`Faction` as the faction that fields it, so a Chinese or Saudi Tanya changes no overlay: without that, China and Saudi
Arabia would have joined her sprite groups and her pistols would have lost their original sound in every game (the
same problem Hold the Ferry Line solved with an allied Tanya side).

- **Glass Island** (`glass-island`, China, Commander Lin Haoran): Haitan Network's terrain, ore cleared, the plateau
  cut off from the lowland by a tidal channel with one causeway. Lin's team (Tanya, a network specialist, a Red Spear)
  lands on the west beach. Six sensor masts (`naradr`, zones of 6 cells): the bluff and causeway masts sweep (6 on / 14
  off, 8 / 14), the ridge mast never sleeps but leaves a strip along the cliff, the gate mast sweeps 8 / 12 (or go
  round by the south coast), and the vault's two masts take turns (10 / 14, offset 12). The specialist deploys within
  4 cells of the core node (`gatech`) to copy the logs; a patrol boat then comes to the north cove, inside the north
  vault mast's zone. "Stay out of the sensor masts' sight" allows one trip on normal (two on easy, none on hard);
  every trip, and every mast destroyed, sends six hunters from the nearest garrison to that mast. The boat leaves at
  first light (8:00; 12:00 on easy, 6:24 on hard). Losing Tanya, the specialist or the whole team fails the mission.
- **Black Platform** (`black-platform`, Saudi Arabia, Commander Turki Al-Zahrani): Bab al-Mandab's map frame painted
  as open sea with concrete deck modules on catwalks (the tileset's pavement tiles; road in Classic). A nine-man
  boarding team (Tanya, two Falcons without their air strike, three riflemen, an anti-tank soldier, two engineers) and
  no reinforcements. The four jammer generators (`gapowr`) carry the Iron
  Curtain's `invulnerability` condition (an event at 0:00) until an engineer takes the curtain control room
  (`nairon`, 2000 HP here); then charges on all four stop the jamming: the map is revealed, Orion lands at the north
  dock, then at the west and east docks every 40 seconds, and two gunboats arrive; hold the room for two minutes.
  Two deck scanners (`naradr`, 4 cells, 8 / 8, taking turns) watch the control deck's sides and the catwalks to the
  wings; a trip brings the deck crew, and staying clear of them is a bonus objective. The charges must be in before
  Orion's relief flotilla arrives (9:00; 13:30 on easy, 7:12 on hard). Losing Tanya, the control room, or both
  engineers before the capture fails the mission.
- **Every Light On** (`every-light-on`, Saudi Arabia, Colonel Badr Al-Shammari): Straits Shield's terrain, the island
  chain's central landmass. A forward base with $10,000 and a small force. Destroy three radar domes (`amradr`,
  1800 HP here, each behind two Tesla coils, a sentry gun and a garrison), then capture the Chronosphere (`gacsph`,
  invulnerable, its capture sealed by a condition until the strike) with an engineer. Four fronts are mission events
  heard on the radio: Lin's picture (the map revealed while the radar relay `PictureRelay` stands; the fog returns if
  it falls), Turki's platform (1:15, radio only), Kemal's northern lane (gunboats every 110 s from 2:30 until the lane
  closes at 8:00), Nabil's towns (at 10:00 the seal lifts and a strike team of two engineers and two Bradleys lands
  near the hub). Hub columns attack the base every 150 seconds from 4:00; two guard towers and a TOW post cover the
  base. Losing the construction yard fails the mission.

Scripted players (`tools/mission-probe/Scripted.cs`, plans `stealth`, `boarding`, `finale`) finish all three on easy,
normal and hard: Glass Island in about 2:40 without an alarm, Black Platform in about 4:30, Every Light On in about
10:30 (the strike waits for the towns; on hard an all-in army has lost the yard to the hub columns). They play like a
careful human who knows the map, so they show that each chapter can be finished and where it bites, not how hard it
feels. The deadlines leave a first-time player about three times (Glass Island) and twice (Black Platform) the
scripted time on normal; an idle player loses Glass Island at 8:00 and Black Platform at 9:00.

### Act IV · Aftershocks

Two more chapters on the same features, one per faction, on separate coasts (rule 3 of the story bible). They need no
new simulation code: map rules (conditions on existing traits) and mission events do the rest.

- **Sweet Water** (`sweet-water`, Iran, Colonel Arash Tavakoli): Jizan Corridor's terrain with a painted coast and dock,
  a salt flat (the tileset's sand) and the salt road across it. A road camp with a small column. Three roadblocks
  (`RoadblockWestGun`, `RoadblockMidGun`, `RoadblockEastGun`: a sentry gun on the road with a squad and armour; the east
  one has a tesla coil) must be destroyed before the army water trucks are due (10:00; 15:00 easy, 8:00 hard); then
  three trucks (`sapc`, speed 70 here) drive from `TruckEntry` to the town `Cistern` (two must arrive; one on easy). The
  cell's plant: engineers capture the pumps (`WaterPlant`, a refinery placed without its free harvester) and the power
  station north of it (`PlantPower`; taking it starves the cell's tesla coils of power). Both, and the vault
  (`CoilVault`), carry an `intact` condition (granted at 0:00: `DamageMultiplier` 0), so they can only be taken; the
  vault is also `sealed` (its `Capturable` needs `!sealed`) until 45 seconds after the plant is retaken (`vault-open`).
  Raiders from `RaidCamp` hit the camp every 3 minutes from 5:00 until the plant is retaken; afterwards recovery
  parties land at the `Dock` every 40 seconds and two gunboats come to `DockWater`. Losing the construction yard fails
  the mission.
- **Ghosts on the Glass** (`ghosts-on-the-glass`, Israel, Lieutenant Colonel Maya Ronen): the Bab al-Mandab map's frame
  repainted as a bay with a land bridge across its head, the city on the west shore, the cell's cape on the east. Nine
  contacts (`Contact1`-`Contact9`, `htnk` on land and `hyd` at sea) belong to a side named Contacts, hostile to the
  player and allied to Orion, so the player's units fire on every one of them. Six are phantoms (condition `phantom`,
  granted at 0:00); `contact_rules()` in `prepare-content.py` gives the two actor types, through map rules: no weapons
  while `phantom`, a tenth of the damage while unverified, and, once verified, a cloak nothing detects (detection type
  `Ghost`) and no damage. Each contact is a sensor of radius 3 with no sweep: a unit of the player's within three cells
  verifies it (`sensor-tripped`): its amber ring turns pale (phantom) or red (real). A phantom destroyed before that
  (`killed`) grants `struck` to `ContactPicture`, a waypoint that map rules give `Health`, a `HitShape` and
  `KillsSelf@struck`; the primary objective "Fire on nothing you have not verified" protects it, so a strike on nothing
  loses the mission (and the objective's Locate button points at the bay, not at which contacts are phantoms). Three
  wider sensors (radius 9, on waypoints) fire `warn-*` events for the radio before a column reaches the contacts on the
  city road and the bay head. Destroying the three relay towers (`naradr`; 16:00 on normal) grants `spoof-off` to every
  contact (all phantoms fade, real ones turn red) and reveals the map; the strike group then lands and the harbour must
  hold 2:30. The families' three shelters (`gapile`, a non-combatant Civilians side) must stand. Orion lands on the beach
  north of the harbour every 2:30 from 4:00 until the relays fall. Bonus: verify all nine contacts. The generator checks
  that every phantom starts beyond twelve cells of every armed unit and gun of the player's.

### Mission features (JSON in `content/missions.json`)

```jsonc
{
  "sensors": [{ "actor": "MastBluff", "radius": 6, "sweep": [6, 14], "offset": 0 }],  // sweep: [on, off] s; none: always on
  "events": [{
    "id": "alarm-MastBluff",
    "when": { "detected": "MastBluff" },   // or {"time": s}, {"objective": n}, {"failed": n}, {"detected": "*"},
                                           //    {"killed": "Actor"}, {"event": "id"}; the first field set applies
    "delay": 0, "repeat": 0, "until": null, // seconds after the trigger; repeat every n s until the "until" event fires
    "spawn": [{ "owner": "Orion Group", "at": "GarrisonWest", "to": "MastBluff", "types": ["e2", "dog"] }],
    "reveal": [{ "at": "ExtractionCove", "radius": 6, "seconds": 0 }],  // no "at": the whole map; 0 s: until concealed
    "conceal": ["picture"],                // remove another event's reveals
    "grant": [{ "condition": "invulnerability", "actors": ["GenNW"] }], // ExternalCondition on named actors
    "revoke": [{ "condition": "invulnerability", "actors": ["GenNW"] }]
  }],
  "objectives": [
    { "kind": "undetected", "targets": ["MastBluff"], "allowed": 1 },   // fails after more trips than allowed (+1 easy, -1 hard)
    { "kind": "squad", "targets": ["Engineer1", "Engineer2"], "count": 1, "until": 0 }, // fails below count alive
    { "kind": "capture", "targets": ["Chronosphere"], "requires": [0], "after": ["towns-clear"] },
    { "kind": "reach", "target": "ExtractionCove", "requires": [0], "limit": 480 }  // fails if not complete by 8:00
  ]
}
```

- A sensor's actor shows its zone through its rules: `sensor_rules(kind, radius)` in `prepare-content.py` adds two
  `ExternalCondition`s (`sensor-on`, `sensor-tripped`, granted by the host) and three always-visible
  `WithRangeCircle`s (amber while sweeping, dim while asleep, red once tripped). The desktop draws them as
  annotations; the browser snapshot captures them in a pass the page draws over the shroud (batch flag `above`).
  A sensor trips once, when a player unit (not a building) is within its radius while it is on; a dead sensor sees
  nothing.
- Enemy spawns follow the difficulty (easy: every other unit; hard: half as many again); the player's do not. Enemy
  units attack-move to `to`, else to the trigger's site (the tripped sensor or the lost actor), else to the nearest
  player unit; the player's move to `to`.
- Any objective can have a deadline (`limit`, game seconds from the start, gated or not; half as long again on easy,
  a fifth shorter on hard): it fails if it is not complete by then, and its progress shows the time left.
- Every objective can wait for objectives (`requires`) and events (`after`); a timed objective behind a gate counts
  from the moment it opens. `protect`, `squad` and `undetected` complete with the mission, or when their `until`
  objective completes. The page shows a waiting objective with a lock (`ui.mission.objectives[n].waiting`).
- `ui.mission.events` lists the fired event ids in order; radio calls can use `{ "event": "<id>" }`.
- `websim missiontest` modes: `success`, `failure` (the first protected actor, else the first protect objective's
  targets), `squad` (the squad is lost) and `detected` (a unit walks into every zone: the alarms must fire and the
  stay-undetected objective must fail, ending the mission when it is primary); `--difficulty` sets the level.

All thirteen missions are available immediately.

## Local generation and validation

From `RTSAI-WebGame`:

```powershell
python tools/prepare-content.py
dotnet build sim/OpenRA.WebSim.Native -c Release -p:WebProfile=public
tools/public.ps1 -NoBuild
& C:/Users/Admin/.dotnet/dotnet.exe publish sim/OpenRA.WebSim.Browser -c Release -p:WebProfile=public -o data/public/wasm-interp
npm --prefix web run build
node server/serve.mjs
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:3490/?profile=public&runtime=interp`. The public profile requires no original RA2 files. Generated packs, runtime output and sprite caches are local build artifacts.

Native regression commands accept an absolute extracted VFS root:

```powershell
websim.exe contenttest --root <absolute-vfs-root> --faction america
websim.exe missiontest --root <absolute-vfs-root> --mission haitan-network --mode success
websim.exe missiontest --root <absolute-vfs-root> --mission haitan-network --mode failure
node tools/fidelity.mjs --profile public --runtime interp --scenario scenarios/mission-haitan-network.json --ticks 300
node tools/content-browser-test.mjs
```

The browser regression currently targets local port3505. Native completion tests exercise scripted objective transitions and actual convoy/amphibious routes; they remove enemy combat units to isolate objective regression from combat balance. Manual difficulty and balance playtesting remains useful.

This work is local only. The website gateway is `RTSAI-Web` (`alibad/RTSAI-Web`); `/play` forwards to the Vercel game deployment. This browser checkout has no configured Git remote. Publishing requires a separate explicit deployment request.
