# Public browser release: 15 October 2026, less garbage per tick

The owner: "let's do 1 and 4", that is, one scripted, checked release for /play and less garbage during play; then
"yes push all and deploy". This is the first release made with `tools/release-play.mjs`.

## What players get

Play makes less garbage, so the worker pauses less often to collect it, and the tab spends less CPU. Each figure below
is 20 seconds of play in the browser, using the same page and data with only the runtime changed. In both the
skirmish and the battle, the camera was on the player's units.
- Skirmish: 11.2 MB/s before, 5.0 MB/s after.
- Original-art battle: 15 MB/s before, 13 MB/s after.

How it was done is in [PERFORMANCE.md](PERFORMANCE.md) under "Garbage per tick". Nothing else changes: no new
features, the simulation is unchanged, and the data is unchanged.

# Second update, 15 October ~03:20 Doha: towers no longer stop the match

## What players get

A match no longer freezes the first time a tesla coil or prism tower charges. Before this fix the simulation stopped
for good with "File not found: btespow.wav" (or `bpripow.wav`), in both isometric and Classic mode, with no recovery.
- Any match where Cuba, Libya, Iraq or Russia builds a tesla coil, or America, Germany, England, France or Korea
  builds a prism tower, was hit. A Russia AI builds one and stopped the match about 6:40 in (Classic: about 5:00).
- A map from the editor with a cow, a civilian prop, a fence or sandbags stopped on its first tick.
- It started with runtime `f21697d` (7 Oct): every public data root since then lacked those two files. Desktop and
  the native websim skip a missing file, so only the browser stopped.
- Towers now charge silently, and map-editor actors without a sound are quiet, until the mod ships their sounds.

## Sources

- **Page:** main `4bc156a`. It is the live page `812b7fe` plus these two kinds of change, with no `web/` change:
  - the data-side fix, cherry-picked from `fix/tesla-sound-main` (the Act IV session): `7122185`, `ba20fb1`,
    `1023432` and `c34ac1d`. `tools/prepare-content.py` now checks every sound a public game can play, including
    `AttackTesla.ChargeAudio`, which the old audit missed. It drops the sounds the build lacks; a voice line keeps a
    0.05 s silent clip instead, because removing it makes the engine throw "Can't find Die in voice pool".
  - the release tool change `4bc156a` (below).
- **Runtime:** `rt-76b11471c4dc/` is unchanged. The runtime fix comes in the next release (`f33c6f8`: the
  browser's audio gate logs a missing sound once and skips it). The native sound tests come with it.
- **Data:** new root `data-f2d5ece96d44/`, built from the same inputs as `data-d1ecc8196c0a/` (mod `de43103b…`, RTSAI-Mod
  `b6794bd`). It differs only in each mode's `web-bundle.pack` and `sprites/index.json` (the bundle id), plus the silent
  clip in `web-audio.pack`. The art, the other audio and every sprite sheet are byte-identical.
- **Page catalog:** `web/src/data/factions.json` is gitignored, and a fresh checkout lacks it, so the first prepare in
  a new worktree failed. This release copied the main checkout's file, the one the live page was built with (catalog
  `2026-10-04.1`). That keeps the page identical. `node tools/sync-catalog.mjs` would give `2026-10-07.1`, with unit
  stories and a new Hezbollah tagline: a page change for a later release.

# Third update, 15 October: Act IV, the epilogue, villages, a co-commander for missions

The owner: "spend all the time you need to do these / do 1, 3, chapter 3-5, Civilian and village art, Story: an
epilogue, Iran and Israel, a sequel hook / test via video walkthrough on Phones, Safari, Firefox, desktop".

## What players get

- **Act IV, Aftershocks.** Two new playable chapters after Act III. Orlov is gone, and the Directorate's last cells are
  racing for the coil caches that could reopen the echo. Both places are fictional, the enemy is always the
  Directorate, and the two nations never share a battlefield.
  - *Sweet Water* (Iran, Colonel Arash Tavakoli). Clear a salt road before the water trucks are due, bring them to the
    town cistern, take the desalination plant and its power station with engineers, and take the coil cache in the
    vault whole.
  - *Ghosts on the Glass* (Israel, Lieutenant Colonel Maya Ronen). A harbour city's picture fills with nine contacts,
    and most of them are phantoms. Verify every contact before firing on it, destroy the relays that paint the lie, and
    keep the shelters standing.
  - Each chapter has a briefing film cut around real footage of the mission, plus voiced radio calls: 30 new calls in
    one Chatterbox voice per character, every one Whisper-checked at 0.8 or better.
- **The epilogue, "1946."** A 34-second film after the last act. Lantern pins the photograph of the Chronosphere's
  first test to her wall, and tonight there is one more man in it. A locked "Part II · The Retoucher" card follows.
- **Villages and civilians.** Six civilian looks, drawn without flags or insignia, panic when under fire and wander when
  idle, and each has a selection-panel portrait. Nine village buildings have damaged states, and the houses and well
  have ruins. Random maps now place villages at least 18 cells from any start position, and Lights in the Water's
  crews are civilians.
- **The co-commander plays campaign missions.** A mission planner reads the objectives, sensors, events and named
  places. It handles stealth crossings, verifies contacts before firing, makes engineer captures, and balances army and
  economy in base missions. In Auto it won 27 of 39 runs, up from 3, and it never fired on an unverified phantom.
- **Phones, Safari, Firefox.**
  - A lost WebGL context now restores the battlefield.
  - The phone landscape HUD covers 24% of the screen, down from 45%.
  - Tap targets are 44 px, and a pinch no longer zooms the page.
  - iOS films get a Play button, and sound starts on touch-end.
  - The briefing stays in view.
  - Esc ends Repair and Sell, and Repair and Sell show the right keys.
  - The menus no longer overlap.
  - Firefox boot is about 3 s faster, because maps load on demand.
- **Towers.** A missing sound no longer stops the browser simulation (the runtime gate, `f33c6f8`), on top of the data
  hotfix in the second update.
