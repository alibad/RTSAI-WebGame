The people, tools and sources behind the game

Credits & licenses

RTS AI is built by the RTS AI contributors on OpenRA, with project-made artwork, procedural sound effects, synthetic character voices and generated instrumental music.

Game and engine

OpenRA and the RTS AI mod carry GNU GPL version 3 licenses. Engine contributors are credited in OpenRA's AUTHORS file. Content has its own provenance and license records.

Project voices and music

The character voices are synthetic. The recorded voice-production process uses generated speaker references rather than recordings of real people. Native-speaker review is still pending for some faction lines.

See the audio provenance documentation and download the project audio inventory, including filenames, SHA-256 hashes and pinned voice-model revisions.

The inventory covers project audio available to both modes; a particular browser build may use a subset.

Artwork and typography

The modern faction sprites and cameos come from the project's model and render pipeline. Cameo labels use Kenney Pixel under CC0 with a project-drawn diagonal R. The art provenance documentation records individual asset and flag sources.

Original Command & Conquer content

Where present in the isometric browser build, original Command & Conquer art, unit voices and sounds are used under EA's free, non-commercial modding guidelines. EA has not endorsed and does not support this product. No Command & Conquer music is included. This content is separate from the project audio inventory.
